Which NAM files work?

Only the verified mono, 48 kHz A2 Lite layout: schema 0.7.0, 3 channels and 1,871 weights. An A2 container with one compatible Lite profile also works; that profile is selected automatically. The converter checks the complete supported layout. Other architectures and A2 Full are rejected. It cannot shrink or retrain an unsupported capture.

No installation, account or internet connection required. This tool converts captures; it does not flash firmware or contact the device.
